Frequently asked questions

How does the translation work?

Translation runs through the free companion app on your phone (iPhone and Android). Choose two languages, speak normally and you hear the translation in your ear. It needs a mobile data or Wi-Fi connection. Music and calls work like any Bluetooth earbuds, no app needed.

Will they fall off when I move?

No. The flexible clip hugs the edge of your ear without entering the ear canal, so it stays secure while running, cycling or walking – and it fits ears of all sizes.

Is AVERA Lingo a hearing aid?

No, it is not a medical device. Paired with a sound amplifier app (for example Google Sound Amplifier), it can make voices and sounds around you louder and clearer. If you have hearing loss, please speak to a hearing professional.

How long does the battery last?

About 4 hours per charge and up to 20 hours in total with the charging case. The LED display shows the exact battery level, and a full charge via USB-C takes around 1 to 1.5 hours.

Which languages are supported?

132 languages and dialects, including Spanish, French, Italian, German, Portuguese, Greek, Turkish, Arabic, Japanese, Chinese and Korean.

What’s in the box?

Two open-ear clip earbuds, the LED charging case, a USB-C cable and a user guide. Every AVERA Lingo comes with a 1-year warranty and our 30-day home trial.
